A recent episode of the “AI & I” podcast, covering an HBR piece, argues that fostering trust is more effective than strict policy for getting companies to actually adopt AI. The core idea is that when employees and leaders trust the technology—and feel safe experimenting with it—they’re far more likely to integrate it into their workflows than if they’re just following top-down rules. This matters because many organizations are currently stalled by compliance-heavy approaches that create friction and fear, slowing down innovation.
