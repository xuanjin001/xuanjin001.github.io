AI Will Hit a Wall in 2026, if nothing changes. - YouTube Link to heading

A growing number of experts, including physicist Sabine Hossenfelder, are warning that the current approach to AI development will hit a hard wall by 2026. The core problem is that we’re running out of high-quality training data, and simply scaling up existing models with more computing power is no longer yielding the same dramatic improvements. This matters because the entire tech industry is betting billions on continuous AI breakthroughs, and a sudden plateau could trigger a major market correction and force a fundamental rethink of how we build intelligence. Without a genuine theoretical breakthrough—like a new architecture or a way to learn from far less data—we might be looking at the end of the “bigger is better” era in AI. source
