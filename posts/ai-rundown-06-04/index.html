Researchers from the Chinese Academy of Sciences have developed a new test that uses Raman spectroscopy—a laser-based technique—to quickly determine whether bacteria are susceptible to phages (viruses that kill bacteria). This matters because antibiotic resistance is a growing crisis, and phage therapy is a promising alternative, but current testing methods are too slow for real-time clinical decisions. The new “Ramanome” approach can deliver results in minutes rather than hours or days, potentially allowing doctors to rapidly match the right phage to a patient’s infection. This breakthrough could accelerate the use of personalized phage treatments, offering a faster, more precise weapon against drug-resistant superbugs. source
