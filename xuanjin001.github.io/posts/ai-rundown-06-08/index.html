TLT’s AI Brief: June 2026 | TLT LLP Link to heading

TLT’s June 2026 AI Brief rounds up the latest legal and regulatory shifts in artificial intelligence, focusing on how new data privacy rules are reshaping how companies train their models. The key takeaway is that regulators are cracking down on the use of personal data scraped from social media and video platforms without explicit consent, with Twitter and Vimeo cookies specifically flagged as potential compliance risks. This matters because businesses relying on AI tools now face stricter obligations to audit their data sources or risk hefty fines. In context, this continues a global trend where privacy laws like the GDPR are finally catching up to the rapid, often messy rollout of generative AI. source

This matters because it represents a massive leap from traditional keyword searches to a fully interactive, task-completing assistant built right into the world’s most popular search engine. Will A.I. replace auto mechanics? | June 5, 2026 - YouTube Link to heading

A recent CNN 10 segment tackled the big question of whether AI is coming for auto mechanics’ jobs. The piece explores how AI-powered diagnostic tools and even robotic systems are starting to handle tasks like identifying engine problems or performing routine maintenance, which could drastically change the garage floor. This matters because the auto repair industry employs hundreds of thousands of people, and while AI might not fully replace human mechanics anytime soon, it will likely shift their role from hands-on tinkering to overseeing smart machines. As cars become more like computers on wheels, the mechanic of the future may need to be part technician and part data analyst. source
